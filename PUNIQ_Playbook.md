## Chapter 1 — Corporate Strategy

### 1. Company Positioning

PUNIQ is an asset-light B2B software company that monetizes the commercial real estate optimization lifecycle. It operates at the precise intersection of construction project delivery and building operations.

**Core Tagline**: "One voice. One brain. One building."

**AI Positioning**: Artificial intelligence is not a feature PUNIQ markets. It is a structural component of how PUNIQ delivers methodology and service. Present in the architecture from day one, not announced as an upgrade.

---

### 2. The Three-Product Portfolio

The portfolio is strictly sequenced. P2 and P3 are the entry mechanism. P1 is the recurring revenue engine. P1 cannot exist without P2 or P3 running first. This sequence is non-negotiable.

#### Product 2 — The Data Auditor
- **Deployment**: On-premises. Buildings with an existing BMS.
- **Problem**: Existing BMS infrastructure is siloed, multi-vendor, inconsistently named, and unreadable as a unified system.
- **Four Outputs**: Unified dashboard · Documented data map · Persistent local data layer (72-hour buffer) · Local control interface
- **Strategic note**: P2 is not a project tool. It is a permanent infrastructure installation. The client pays for the project solution. PUNIQ retains the cloud data stream as the P1 on-ramp.

#### Product 3 — The Infrastructure Blueprint (Edge-BMS)
- **Deployment**: On-premises. Buildings with zero existing BMS (greenfield).
- **Problem**: No automation baseline exists. P3 builds it from scratch.
- **Four Outputs**: Same as P2 — built from zero rather than unified from chaos.
- **Strategic note**: Correct entry for greenfield projects and direct property owner engagements with no prior infrastructure.

#### Product 1 — The AI Optimization SaaS
- **Deployment**: Cloud subscription. Activated only after P2 or P3 is live.
- **Core distinction**: P2/P3 give the building a voice. P1 gives it a brain. P2 answers 'What is happening now?' P1 answers 'What should be, what will be, and what is wrong that you cannot see yet.'
- **Four Functions**:
  1. Benchmarking — compares against comparable assets
  2. Forecasting — projects energy costs, carbon position, equipment degradation
  3. Anomaly Detection — identifies patterns 30–60 days before failure
  4. Regulatory Compliance Output — GEG, EU Taxonomy, CSRD (optional add-on)
- **Critical boundary**: P1 NEVER sends direct execution commands to physical equipment. It proposes. A licensed human reads the Insight Card and acts locally through P2 or P3. This boundary is architecturally enforced and legally non-negotiable.

### 3. The Trojan Horse Entry Strategy

PUNIQ bypasses the slow property owner sales cycle by entering through construction actors who have an immediate deadline problem.

- **Path A** — Engineering firm pays PUNIQ to deploy P2/P3 and deliver clean handover
- **Path B** — General contractor pays PUNIQ to design architecture for smart-building bid
- **Path C** — Direct property owner engagement from the start

**The SaaS Pivot**: After 30+ days of building data collected, PUNIQ meets directly with property owner and demonstrates real intelligence about their specific building. The ask: paid 30-day trial at full subscription price. No free trials. No discounts.

---

### 4. The Financial Architecture

- **Fixed upfront**: P2/P3 project fee — one-time per project
- **Recurring SaaS**: P1 monthly/annual subscription — paid by property owner directly
- **Hardware Shield**: PUNIQ NEVER buys, stocks, ships, or warrants physical equipment. PUNIQ produces a Bill of Materials and hands it to the contractor. Hardware never appears on PUNIQ's balance sheet.

---

### 5. The Compliance Shield

- P1 is legally classified as an **Advisory Optimization Service**. It monitors, analyzes, forecasts, and recommends. It does not control.
- Every actionable output is delivered as an Insight Card to a licensed human operator.
- **§71a GEG aligned** · **Outside BSI C5 and NIS2 scope** · **EU Data Act (2024) tailwind**

---

### 6. Churn Prevention Architecture

- **Benchmarking**: Owner has a performance score. Cancelling = returning to not knowing.
- **Forecasting**: By month 6, facility manager's budget process depends on P1 forecasts.
- **Anomaly Detection**: Once P1 catches one real problem, owner permanently carries the question: "What is happening right now that I cannot see?"
- **Regulatory Output**: Cancelling means hiring a consultant at a cost likely exceeding the annual subscription.
- **Compounding Data Effect**: Every month P1 runs, the building's dataset deepens. Switching cost is the accumulated intelligence of the building itself.

## Chapter 3 — Frozen Product Definitions

### Pre-Assessment — Building Intelligence Readiness Report
- **Cost**: Free. Always. No exceptions.
- **Output**: P2/P3 ratio per system, Tier coverage map, indicative fee range
- **Disqualification**: Does not exist. Every building produces a ratio.

### P2/P3 — Building Intelligence Foundation

**Mission**: Deploy a permanent, vendor-neutral intelligence foundation. Give the building a voice. Create the on-ramp for P1.

**Pricing**: One-time fixed project fee. Perpetual license — client owns the deployed version at handover forever.

**Four Deliverables**:
1. **Unified Dashboard** — Single vendor-neutral operational view of all building systems regardless of protocol or vendor. Real-time data. Configurable threshold alerts. Mobile responsive.
2. **Documented Data Map** — Every data point named, sourced, normalized, and verified. 10 mandatory fields. PUNIQ's primary market differentiator — no legacy integrator produces this.
3. **Local Data Layer** — Real-time collection and normalization. 72-hour connectivity buffer. Continuous outbound stream to PUNIQ cloud. Not a local archive.
4. **Local Control Interface** — Human-initiated on-premises control only. Three roles: Viewer (read-only) / Operator (manual control) / Administrator (full config). Full audit log, 10-year retention.

**Deployment Acceptance Protocol (DAP)**:
- 5 criteria: Dashboard live · Data map delivered · Local layer confirmed · Control interface tested · Liability boundary signed
- Both parties sign → triggers final payment

**Post-handover**: Outbound-only. Nothing pushed back remotely. Ever. Updates require physical on-site presence.

### P1 — Building AI Intelligence

**Mission**: Give the building a brain. Turn clean operational data into continuous intelligence.

**Activation conditions** (all 5 required):
1. Local layer live 7+ days post-handover
2. HVAC and energy metering confirmed flowing to cloud
3. No cloud gap exceeding 72 hours
4. P1 contract signed
5. Minimum one Administrator account active

**Insight Cards**:
- Format: Title · System/location · Data evidence · Recommended action · Estimated impact · Priority
- Delivery: Dashboard + email to Administrator
- Priority tiers: **Urgent** (act within 48hrs) · **Recommended** (act within 2 weeks) · **Informational** (awareness)
- Escalation: Urgent cards unacted after 48hrs escalate to Administrator and Viewer
- Expiry: Active until acted upon, dismissed, or deferred — never auto-expires

**Contract**: 12-month minimum. Auto-renewal unless 30 days written notice.

**SLA**: 99.5% monthly uptime (Year 1). Max 72-hour data gap before degraded mode.

**Human-in-the-Loop**: P1 never sends direct execution commands. Advisory only. Human evaluates and acts locally through P2/P3. Architectural, not policy.

**The chain**: P1 recommends → human decides → human acts on P2/P3 locally → P2/P3 executes.

## Chapter 4 — Key Product Definition Decisions (Selected)

### Data Architecture
- Local layer: Real-time collection, normalization, 72-hour buffer only
- Cloud layer: Primary storage, P1 analytics engine, all historical data
- Data flow: Continuous outbound from building to PUNIQ cloud from day one
- Security posture: Outbound-only. Nothing pushed back remotely. Ever.

### The Value Bridge Narrative (for SaaS pivot)
> "What you paid for was the foundation — a unified readable local layer that gives your building a voice. What we offer now is the brain — continuous AI intelligence telling you what your building should do, what it will cost next month, and what is about to go wrong. The foundation works without the brain. The brain makes the foundation worth ten times more."

### Alert vs Insight Card
- **Alert**: Single data point crosses fixed human-configured rule. Alert fatigue risk. No prediction.
- **Insight Card**: AI pattern detection. Learns building's normal. Predictive 30–60 days ahead. Cost-justified actions.

### Benchmarking
- Activates at minimum 5 comparable buildings in portfolio
- Presented as percentile ranking
- Metrics: Energy Use Intensity (kWh/m²/yr) · Carbon intensity (kgCO₂e/m²/yr) · System efficiency ratios

## Chapter 5 — Data Universe (Summary)

### Tier 1 — Core (Day 1)

**HVAC** (40–60% of building energy):
- Zone temperature (actual + setpoint) — read + control
- AHU status, supply/return air temp, fan speed — read + control
- Chiller/boiler/heat pump status and load — read + control
- CO2 concentration per zone (ppm) — read + ventilation control
- Relative humidity per zone (%) — read + control
- Valve positions (heating/cooling coils) — read + control
- HVAC energy consumption (kWh) — read
- FCU status, valve %, fan speed — read + control

**Electrical & Energy**:
- Total building energy (kWh) — real-time + cumulative
- Energy per floor/zone/circuit — read + load shedding
- Peak demand (kW) — read
- Power factor, voltage, current per phase — read
- Energy cost (tariff-based) — calculated real-time
- Carbon intensity — via grid data API

**Lighting**: On/off per zone · Dimming level · Occupancy sensor · Energy (kWh)

**Water & Plumbing**: Total consumption · Flow rate · Hot water temp · Leak detection · Tank level

**Metering**: Main electricity · Gas · Energy Use Intensity · Carbon emissions (kgCO₂e)

**Fire & Safety**: Read-only. No control capability. Legally protected.

### Tier 2 — Extended (Where Present)
- Access control & security
- Building envelope & external environment
- Facility & maintenance data

### Tier 3 — Ambition Roadmap
- Elevators · Renewable energy & storage · EV charging · Advanced IAQ · Digital Twin
